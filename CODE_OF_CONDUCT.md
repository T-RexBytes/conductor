# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Tierly to be a welcoming place for everyone. We pledge to make participation respectful
and free from harassment, regardless of a person's background or identity.

## Our standards

### Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus discussion on the work and its impact.
- Take responsibility and correct mistakes when asked.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or other project work.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would find intimidating or abusive.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and other project
communication. It also applies in public spaces when someone is representing Tierly or the project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing this code. They may remove,
edit, or reject comments and other contributions that violate it. Maintainers will explain
enforcement decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, Sourish Panda (GitHub `@T-RexBytes`), privately by opening a security
advisory at <https://github.com/T-RexBytes/tierly/security/advisories/new>. Only maintainers can
see a private advisory. You can also contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/T-RexBytes). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will choose a response that fits the conduct and its impact.

### Correction

For a minor, isolated issue, the maintainer may explain what was inappropriate and ask the person
to correct it.

### Warning

For repeated or more serious conduct, the maintainer may issue a clear warning and explain what
must change.

### Temporary ban

For continued or serious violations, the maintainer may temporarily restrict participation in
project spaces.

### Permanent ban

For a pattern of violations, harassment, or a serious breach of trust, the maintainer may
permanently bar participation in project spaces.
